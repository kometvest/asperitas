Synthetic Biology

Validating computational enzyme designs through rapid cell-free expressions and targeted genome modifications in our wet lab facilities.

Wet Lab & Validation

The Wet Lab validates AI predictions. Utilizing advanced bioengineering instruments, we translate data predictions into physically validated organisms and chemical structures.

Our molecular focus covers genome editing, biological logic circuits, metabolic precursors, chloroplast integration, and rapid prototyping using cell-free systems, bypassing traditional cell-culturing overhead to verify enzyme models instantly.

Genome Editing

Deploying CRISPR-Cas9, base editors, and target knock-in systems to write engineered code directly into host cells.

Plant Bioengineering

Directing gene constructs to chloroplasts and vacuole matrices to modify leaf coloration and sustain bioluminescence pathways.

Cell-Free Prototyping

Utilizing rapid DNA assembly and cell-free protein expressions to screen and prototype enzyme catalogs in hours.

Scalable Products

Expanding discovery IP into consumer design (luminescent flora), industrial catalysis, therapeutics, and agricultural defense traits.
