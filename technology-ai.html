Biological Metadata & Cheminformatics

Organizing, searching, and analyzing complex biological datasets to build a proprietary intelligence platform that accelerates secondary metabolite discovery.

Proprietary Metadata Platform

Artificial intelligence is an enabling infrastructure, not the final product. Every organism contains critical information beyond its DNA. We capture geographic origin, climate, diet, chemical composition, and ecological interactions alongside genomic sequencing.

Over time, this vast array of contextual data becomes a unique biological intelligence platform. It allows our computational tools to organize and connect biological information, accelerating hypothesis generation and experimental design while remaining firmly grounded in experimental science.

Metadata Organization

Aggregating environmental, geographic, and phenotypic data alongside sequencing to provide full context for discovery.

Cheminformatics

Analyzing secondary metabolites and novel molecular structures found exclusively in uncharted biodiversity.

Knowledge Graphs

Connecting ecological interactions with chemical outputs to predict novel biological functions and adaptations.

Computational Discovery

Using AI models to search massive datasets, generate hypotheses, and guide laboratory experimental design.
