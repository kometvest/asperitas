Mission

Unlocking Biodiversity for Biotechnology.

The Problem

Biotechnology keeps engineering the same narrow biology.

Nature already solved billions of years of chemistry. We keep studying only a tiny fraction of it.

8M+
species on Earth remain functionally unexplored by biotechnology.
Why It Matters

One species can change an entire industry.

"The next one hasn't been discovered yet."

Gila Monster → GLP-1 — the venom compound behind today's leading diabetes and weight-loss drugs
Cone Snail → Ziconotide — a non-opioid painkiller 1,000x more potent than morphine
Jellyfish → GFP — the fluorescent protein that won a Nobel Prize and lit up modern cell biology
Horseshoe Crab → LAL Test — the assay that still screens nearly every injectable drug for contamination
Pacific Yew → Taxol — one of the most widely used chemotherapy drugs in oncology
Thermus aquaticus → Taq Polymerase — the enzyme that makes PCR, and modern genomics, possible

Most biotech companies discover biology from the same public databases. Asperitas sources directly — through a global network of field partners, breeding facilities, and conservation programs that give us continuous, exclusive access to genetic diversity most of the industry can't reach.

Our Platform

A closed-loop platform for biodiversity-derived innovation.

01 Discover Exclusive access to biodiversity through a global sourcing network, continuously expanding the pool of species available for research.
02 Understand Breeding, conservation, and species research turn raw specimens into a living, growing population we can study over time.
03 Engineer Biology becomes product: novel compounds, biologics, and an expanding IP portfolio built from what we discover.
04 Discovery Engine AI-driven discovery and validation accelerate every step, turning biological data into testable hypotheses faster.
05 Biological Intelligence Platform Multi-omics and phenotypic data compound over time into a proprietary intelligence layer no public database can replicate.
